## What happened

One sentence. The symptom as observed, not the theory.

## How to see it

Steps, or the conditions that produce it. Enough that someone else can confirm
it's still true.

## What was actually wrong

The cause, once known — and how it was found, if that was the hard part.
Write "not known yet" rather than a guess dressed up as a finding.

## What was done

The fix, and anything deliberately left undone. Link the issue or commit rather
than restating the diff.
